Delete your account and data

Last updated: 17 November 2026

You can request deletion of your MyGymBro account at any time. Deletion is permanent: it cannot be undone.

1. How to request it

Email danihp6@gmail.com with the subject “MyGymBro account deletion”, from the same address you use to sign in to the app. If the message comes from a different address, we will ask you to confirm before deleting anything.

2. What is deleted

  • The custom exercises you created.
  • All your sessions: sets, weights, repetitions and dates.
  • Your premium purchase record and its verification hash.
  • The preferences the app stores on your device.

3. What is not deleted

  • Your Google account: that is managed by Google, not MyGymBro. If you want it removed, do it from your Google account.
  • The purchase records Google Play keeps for legal and tax obligations. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

4. Timing

We handle the request within 30 days at most and email you once the deletion is done. While it is in progress the account stops accepting new requests from the app.

5. Deleting only part of your data

If you do not want to delete your account, you can delete individual exercises and sessions in the app, or email us to have one exercise or one period removed.
