Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Data controller

Daniel Herrero Pardo, contact address danihp6@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

Category Data Where it comes from
Identity Email address, name and profile picture Sign-in with Google
Training Exercises you create, sets (weight and repetitions), dates and saved sessions What you enter in the app
Premium subscription Whether the purchase is active, the order identifier and a hash of the purchase token (never the full token) Google Play and the purchase verification
Technical Service logs (errors and requests) Amazon Web Services

We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or health data from other applications. We use no third-party analytics and build no advertising profiles of our own.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the service: store your exercises and history and show your statistics.
  • To keep you signed in with your Google account.
  • To verify and maintain the premium purchase state.
  • To protect the service and diagnose failures.

The legal basis is the performance of the service you request and, for technical logs and security, our legitimate interest in keeping it available.

4. Advertising

The free tier shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. The premium purchase removes the ads. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve and measure advertising; you can limit ad personalisation from your device and Google account settings. We never share your training data with advertisers.

5. Who else sees it

We use providers that process the data on our behalf and only to provide the service:

  • Amazon Web Services (eu-west-1, Ireland): database, authentication and catalog image hosting.
  • Google: sign-in (Google Sign-In), purchases (Google Play) and advertising (AdMob).

We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

6. How long

Your training data is kept while your account exists. If you request deletion, it is removed within 30 days at most. Technical logs are kept for a short, limited period.

7. Security

Access to your data requires an authenticated session, traffic is encrypted with HTTPS and provider credentials are stored as secrets, outside the code.

8. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, and object to its processing, by writing to danihp6@gmail.com. For deletion, follow Delete your account and data. You may also complain to the Spanish data-protection authority (aepd.es).

9. Minors

MyGymBro is not aimed at children under 14 and does not knowingly create accounts for them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this same page with its update date.
